Three distinctions to remember

⚡ Epinephrine concentrations

1:1,000 = 1 mg/mL. 1:10,000 = 0.1 mg/mL. One is ten times more concentrated. Always check label, route, and intended indication.

🫁 Anaphylaxis vs. allergy

IM epinephrine is first-line for anaphylaxis. Benadryl may ease hives, but cannot replace epinephrine for airway compromise or shock.

🧠 Paralysis ≠ sedation

Succinylcholine, rocuronium, and vecuronium do not provide pain relief or unconsciousness. Airway, ventilation, sedation, and analgesia are separate responsibilities.
